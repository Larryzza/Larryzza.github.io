Research experience

Ph.D. research, Department of Biostatistics, UCLA

June 2022–present · Advisor: Dr. Gang Li

  • Prediction-powered Cox regression with large language model predictions. A semi-supervised Cox regression method that augments a small labeled sample with predictions, including those from large language models, for a larger unlabeled sample. Under independent censoring, its asymptotic variance is no larger than that of the labeled-only estimator, whether or not the Cox model holds. Applied to NHANES mortality and NACC dementia progression. Manuscript in preparation; Python package in development.
  • Predictive performance evaluation for survival and competing risks. Time-restricted generalized R² and pseudo-R² measures for right-censored competing-risk prediction (Biometrics, in press). A tutorial on survival prediction performance measures and the R package PAmeasures (submitted to Statistics in Medicine).
  • Instrumental variable estimation for censored outcomes. A data-adaptive censored two-stage least squares estimator for semiparametric accelerated failure time models (Statistics in Biosciences, 2026).
  • Historical-control matching for clinical trials. A matching framework that combines propensity scores from structured covariates with large language model embeddings of clinical notes.

Epidemiological modeling and predictive analysis, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University
